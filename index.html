About me

I am currently a Research Fellow at the Singapore University of Technology and Design (SUTD), under the supervision of Prof. CHEE Yeow Meng. I received my Ph.D. from the School of Cyber Science and Engineering, Southeast University, where I was advised by Prof. Bing Li. From 2024 to 2026, I was also a visiting Ph.D. student at the National University of Singapore, advised by Prof. Beng Chin Ooi, where I worked on efficient indexing in NeurDB.

Research

My research focuses on AI-powered autonomous data systems, efficient query processing, and data security. My current research interests include:

  • Self-design and dynamic optimization of learned indexes
  • Secure and privacy-preserving learned indexes
  • Security for AI-powered data systems
