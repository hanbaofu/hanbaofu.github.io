abstract: "Cold standby systems are widely applied in aircraft redundancy design, in which standby components are sequentially activated upon failure of operational components. When time to failure of components follow exponential distributions with identical failure rates, the analytical expression for system failure probability encounters discontinuity points where the denominator approaches zero, yielding physically inadmissible results and making subsequent safety design and optimization infeasible. Classical methods, such as Taylor expansion and interpolation methods, fail to provide closed-form analytical solutions. To address this issue, an exponential probability density function approximation method is developed. The method combines constant, linear, and logarithmic terms to derive analytical expressions.
