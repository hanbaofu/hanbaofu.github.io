abstract: "Common cause failure (CCF) probability serves as a fundamental basis for redundancy system design and safety assessment. Existing CCF probability analysis methods typically rely on system architecture characteristics and operational data, and their predictive capability is limited under conditions involving varying numbers of failed components and different failure cause intensities. To address these challenges, this paper combines the mixed shock model and investigates a theoretical inference method for CCF probability. On this basis, a multi-level Bayesian framework is developed. Through top-down parameter definition and uncertainty quantification, bottom-up inference of CCF probability is achieved, and confidence intervals of the CCF probability for target conditions are obtained.
