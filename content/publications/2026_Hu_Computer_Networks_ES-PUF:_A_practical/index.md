Physically Unclonable Functions (PUFs), as a lightweight intrinsic security mechanism, have been widely adopted for device identification and authentication. However, conventional PUF extraction methods typically require dedicated hardware circuits, limiting flexibility and cost-effectiveness. Inspired by RF-PUF and FD-PUF, which exploit intrinsic signal characteristics to generate PUFs without dedicated hardware, this paper presents a novel method for extracting PUFs directly from Ethernet physical-layer signals, termed Ethernet Signal-based PUFs (ES-PUFs), without the need for specialized security chips or PUF generation circuits.
