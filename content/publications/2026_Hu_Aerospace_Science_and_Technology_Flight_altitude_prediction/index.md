abstract: "Modern aircraft rely on multi-channel flight altitude measurement systems to ensure precise data for flight control. However, harsh operational environments can trigger severe common cause failures (CCF), leading to simultaneous failure across multiple channels. To mitigate the impact of CCF on altitude measurement systems, a diffusion model-based flight altitude prediction and CCF protection method is proposed. Specifically, a four-channel flight altitude measurement model is constructed, incorporating typical CCF modes such as data freezing and data loss derived from historical accident analysis. The proposed method utilizes the forward diffusion process to characterize data uncertainties and a reverse generative process to reconstruct the true altitudes from noisy channel inputs.
